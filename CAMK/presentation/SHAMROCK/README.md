## Numerical Astrophysical Simulations in Warsaw and Lyon

Code for a short presentation on my SMBH accretion disk work in [PLUTO](https://plutocode.ph.unito.it/) at [CAMK](https://www.camk.edu.pl/en/)
up to this point. Prompted by an invitation to a visio with [Jérémy Fensch](https://perso.ens-lyon.fr/jeremy.fensch/) and [Noé Brucy](https://noe.brucy.fr/)
to discuss an internship working on efficient implementation of forcing routines in the [SHAMROCK](https://shamrock-code.github.io/index.html) framework,
studying the shortcomings of such methods in ISM turbulence simulations, especially the effects of introduced spacetime correlations on formation of
gravitationally bound structures and ultimately stars.
