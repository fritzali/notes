				\begin{itemize}
					\item<3-> Calculate Adaptive Disk and Corona Weighted Mean Density Profiles
					\item<4-> Compute Time Smoothed Radial Reference via Exponential Moving Average
							  $$\rho_n \kern-0.5pt = \alpha \tilde{\rho}_n + (1 \kern-0.5pt - \alpha) \kern+0.5pt \rho_{n\kern+0.5pt-1}$$
					\item<5-> Obtain Discriminator $f_\rho$ by Feeding Cell Density into Sigmoid
					\item<6-> Similarly Find $f_\omega$ from Comparing Local Azimuthal to Keplerian Velocity
					\item<7-> Multiply like $f = f_\rho \cdot f_\omega$ for Disk and $1 - f$ for Corona Weights
					\item<8-> Account for Initial Conditions (Truncated Disk \& Hard Binary)
				\end{itemize}
				\item<9-> Parallelize with MPI Reduction Across Entire Domain and Feeding Back to All Ranks
			\end{itemize}
		\end{itemize}
