## Isaac A. Lopez Paredes – *UV Spectroscopy of the Intergalactic Medium*

### Contents Summary
This report reviews ultraviolet spectroscopy as a tool to map baryonic matter across the multiphase intergalactic medium. The text
outlines the underlying radiation mechanisms governing both the cold photoionized gas and the shock heated warm hot intergalactic
medium while characterizing the resulting absorption spectra through features like the Lyman alpha forest. It examines historical
and current space based and ground based instrumentation alongside the plasma diagnostics required to extract physical parameters.
While my own work focuses on the energetic virialized plasmas of galaxy clusters via X-ray spectroscopy, this paper complements
that work by tracing the more diffuse filamentary structures of the cosmic web. A compelling intersection occurs where the
author notes that characterizing the hottest components of the intergalactic medium ultimately requires transitioning into
soft X-ray spectroscopy.

### Noteworthy Aspects
The report features a well structured layout that mimics a professional journal submission. The inclusion of clear schematic
diagrams and spectral plots significantly enhances the visual presentation and supports the readability of the text. The author
also maintains a logical flow between sections by introducing clear introductory remarks for each major topic.

### Possible Improvements
Certain sections suffer from abrupt endings and incomplete sentences that disrupt the overall narrative flow. There are also
occasional typographical errors and awkward phrasing choices within the text that affect the clarity of some descriptions.
Reviewing the formatting of the section headers would ensure total stylistic consistency across the entire document.
