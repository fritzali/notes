## Isaac Alexis López Paredes – *UV Spectroscopy of the Intergalactic Medium*

### Contents Summary
This report reviews ultraviolet spectroscopy as a tool to map baryonic matter across the multiphase intergalactic medium. The text
outlines the underlying radiation mechanisms governing both the cold photoionized gas and the shock heated warm hot intergalactic
medium while characterizing the resulting absorption spectra through features like the Lyman alpha forest. It examines historical
and current space and ground based instrumentation alongside the plasma diagnostics required to extract physical parameters.
While my own work focuses on the energetic virialized plasmas of galaxy clusters via Xray spectroscopy, this paper complements
that work by tracing the more diffuse filamentary structures of the cosmic web. An intersection occurs where the author notes
that characterizing the hottest components of the intergalactic medium ultimately requires transitioning into soft Xray spectroscopy.

### Noteworthy Aspects
The document adopts a clean layout that closely follows the structure of a scientific paper. The author maintains a logical flow between
sections via clear introductory remarks for each major topic. Tackling the missing baryon question give a clear motivation, and the
inventory of old and current contrasted with future missions neatly integrates into the outlook.

### Possible Improvements
The text occasionally suffers from abrupt endings and incomplete sentences that disrupt the overall narrative flow. There are also
some typographical errors and awkward phrasing choices that affect the clarity of descriptions. Including more graphics could
break up some very long passages of pure text.
